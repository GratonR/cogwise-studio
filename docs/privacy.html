Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Cogwise Studio is a private tool used only by the owner of the Cogwise channel (contact: cogwisehtw@gmail.com) to manage his own channels (Cogwise and Beep Melody) on YouTube, Facebook and Instagram.

Data accessed

With the owner's authorization through Google OAuth, the tool uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, read public statistics of the channel's videos, read comments posted on those videos and publish replies. It does not access any other Google data.

Use and storage

This data is used only to publish videos, show the owner his channel statistics and comments, and post the replies he approves. Authorization tokens are stored privately by the owner and are never shared. Public comment text and author names may be shown in the owner's private dashboard. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Google API Services

Cogwise Studio's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube API Services are used under the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Meta (Facebook and Instagram)

Through the Meta Graph API, with the owner's authorization as administrator of his own Facebook Pages (Cogwise, Beep Melody) and their linked Instagram professional accounts, the tool publishes the owner's videos (Reels), reads the statistics of those posts and reads comments posted on them. It does not log in other users, does not access any personal Facebook or Instagram profile, and does not collect data about people who view or comment beyond what Meta shows on the owner's own posts. This data is used only in the owner's private dashboard, is never sold or shared, and is not used for advertising.

Data deletion

The tool stores no data about Facebook or Instagram users. To request deletion of any data related to you (for example the text of a comment you left that appears in the owner's dashboard), write to cogwisehtw@gmail.com with the link to the comment; it is deleted within 30 days. You can also remove the app's access at any time from Facebook Settings > Business integrations.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google account permissions. On request to the contact address, any stored data is deleted.
