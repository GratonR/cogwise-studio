This machine makes ice, then blasts it with heat.
Its secret: it freezes a waterfall.
A pump pours water over an ice-cold plate.
Inside, refrigerant boils and pulls the heat out.
Ice grows layer by layer.
Air and minerals wash away,
so the cubes come out crystal clear.
Once the ice is thick, a probe stops the freeze.
Hot gas floods the plate, and the cubes slip off.
Water, cold, then heat: clear ice.
