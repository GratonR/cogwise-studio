That gap between the bricks isn't a mistake. Your wall is supposed to leak.
Brick and mortar soak up rain like a sponge, and some of it gets through.
Because a brick house wall is really two walls, with air in between.
Outside, a single layer of brick. It only keeps off the weather.
Behind it, an empty gap, about two and a half centimetres, one inch, wide.
Then the real wall, the one holding up your house.
In a storm, wind drives water straight through the bricks.
It trickles down the back of the brick, inside the gap, without touching the inner wall.
At the bottom, a sloped sheet called flashing catches every drop.
Then it slides out through these gaps, left empty on purpose. Weep holes.
Fill them with mortar, and the water stays trapped inside the wall.
Every brick wall leaks. The weep hole is where it cries.
