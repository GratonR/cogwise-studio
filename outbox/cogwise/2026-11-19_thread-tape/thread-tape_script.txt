Wrap this tape the wrong way, and the fitting unwinds it for you.
A turning fitting doesn't just slide over the tape. It drags the tape along with it.
Even screwed tight, metal threads never touch perfectly. Tiny gaps remain.
Those gaps form one spiral tunnel around the pipe, and water follows it out.
This tape is PTFE, the slippery plastic on non-stick pans, thinner than paper.
Squashed into the threads, it plugs the tunnel, and lets them slide deeper.
But as you turn the fitting, its threads rub the tape and pull it around.
Wrapped against that turn, the loose end faces the threads. They catch it.
The tape peels back, rolls into a little rope, and the tunnel stays open.
Wrapped with the turn, that same rub pulls every layer tighter instead.
Looking at the pipe's open end, that means clockwise, the way the fitting goes on.
The fitting always drags the tape its way. So wrap it that way too.
