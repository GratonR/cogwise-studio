Your dishwasher never fills up. It cleans everything with one puddle.
Just four liters, one gallon, in the bottom. A float stops the valve.
A pump sucks that puddle up and blasts it through the spray arms.
The arms have no motor: angled jets push them around, like a sprinkler.
A heater warms it to about sixty Celsius, a hundred forty Fahrenheit.
On every pass, a filter catches the food, so the water stays usable.
Same water: sprayed, caught, pumped again, for many minutes.
Then it drains, rinses with fresh water, drains again, and dries.
Whole cycle: about thirteen liters, three and a half gallons. By hand: up to a hundred liters.
So: one hot puddle, pumped in a loop, filtered on every pass.
